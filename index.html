      Positions from Garmin LiveTrack (watch → phone → cell) and inReach MapShare (satellite). Not an emergency channel: SOS events do not appear here, and a quiet page means only that no new positions have arrived.
      
    
      Updates every 30 seconds while open. #live always opens the current activity.
      
    
      
    
      Data sources.
      Positions, heart rate and effort: Garmin LiveTrack and Garmin inReach MapShare, plus the watch's own activity files (FIT, GPX, TCX).
      Weather: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed
      CC BY 4.0; modeled, not station readings.
      Activities before 2021 use ERA5 reanalysis from the Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S), via Open-Meteo.
      Weather alerts: U.S. National Weather Service (US only).
      Maps: CyclOSM and OpenTopoMap (CC BY-SA) tiles,
      map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), shown with Leaflet.
      Default activity names: public lands from the USGS Protected Areas Database (PAD-US);
      towns from OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
      Trails, streets, trailheads and landmarks in the Timeline: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via the Overpass API.
      Sun and moon times: calculated on this page with formulas after SunCalc (V. Agafonkin, BSD-2-Clause); flat-horizon times, terrain not modeled.
